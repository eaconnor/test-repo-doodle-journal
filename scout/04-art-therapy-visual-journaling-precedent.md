## The clinical-claims line — where this concept must NOT go
Everything above sits inside a clinical/therapeutic literature: art therapy is a licensed profession, REPAT is an intervention *protocol* designed for structured clinical delivery, and the Pizarro/trauma-recovery framing is explicitly about trauma treatment. **An internal Tier-1 prototype journaling app must not claim, imply, or design toward "processes trauma," "therapeutic," "heals," "treats," or any language borrowed from this tradition.** The concept can be *informed by* the idea that images access something words don't; it cannot claim clinical efficacy it has not tested, on a population (internal design/product staff, not clinical subjects) this literature was never validated against.
